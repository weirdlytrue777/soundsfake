Sounds Fake Studio

A simple desktop tool for creators to publish their own original short videos to TikTok - with full control over the caption, who can watch, interactions and disclosures before anything is posted.

What it does

Sounds Fake Studio is a small desktop application made by Sounds Fake, the team behind the Sounds Fake weird-but-true fact shorts (YouTube @soundsfake77, TikTok @soundsfake77). It runs on the creator's own computer, lets the creator sign in with their TikTok account using TikTok Login Kit, and publishes a short video file from their computer to their TikTok profile using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Nothing is ever posted automatically: every post is reviewed and confirmed by the creator on the posting screen.

How it works

  1. Open Sounds Fake Studio on your computer and click Continue with TikTok.
  2. Authorize on TikTok's own page. Sounds Fake Studio asks only for your basic profile (to show who is signed in) and permission to upload and publish videos you choose.
  3. Choose a video from your computer. It is previewed and checked against the maximum length TikTok allows for your account.
  4. Set the details: caption, who can view, comments / Duet / Stitch, and any commercial content disclosure.
  5. Confirm and post. Only after you click Post is the video sent to TikTok. Processing can take a few minutes before it appears on your profile; the app shows the status.

TikTok permissions used

PermissionWhy
user.info.basicShow the signed-in account's display name and avatar so you know which account you are using.
video.publishPublish the video you selected, with the settings you chose, directly to your TikTok profile.
video.uploadOptional: send the selected video to your TikTok inbox as a draft, so you can finish editing and post it inside the TikTok app.

You can disconnect at any time inside the app or in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services.

Content rules

Sounds Fake Studio is for publishing original content you own or have the rights to. It does not copy, download or re-post content from other platforms or other creators, and it does not add watermarks, logos or promotional text to your videos. Please follow the TikTok Community Guidelines.
