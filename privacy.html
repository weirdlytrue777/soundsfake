Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sounds Fake Studio (the "App"), operated by ChiaChing Haung under the name Sounds Fake in Taiwan ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the App to sign in with TikTok and publish videos. In short: we only use your data to post the videos you choose, we keep it on the device that runs the App, and we never sell or share it.

1. Information the App accesses

The App does not access your TikTok password, messages, contacts, followers list, other users' data, or your existing videos' analytics. It does not use cookies for tracking, analytics SDKs or advertising.

2. How the information is stored

  • The App runs locally on the computer (or private server) where it is installed. Tokens and settings are stored there in a file readable only by the operating-system account running the App. They are not sent to any server operated by us.
  • Videos are read from your device and sent directly to TikTok's servers over HTTPS only after you click Post. We do not keep extra copies of your videos.
  • A local log records technical events (for example publish ID and status) for troubleshooting. Tokens and secrets are never written to logs.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone. Information is sent only to TikTok, as needed to perform the post you requested. We may disclose information only if required by law.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept until you disconnect. Disconnecting in the App revokes the token with TikTok and deletes it from the device.
  • You can also revoke access in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services.
  • To request deletion of any information related to you, email weirdlytrue77@gmail.com. We will confirm deletion within 30 days.
  • Videos you published are stored by TikTok and are managed or deleted in TikTok under TikTok's Privacy Policy.

5. Security

We use TikTok's OAuth 2.0 with PKCE and state checks, HTTPS for all API calls, restricted file permissions for stored tokens, and we never embed or expose the App's client secret. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Contact us at weirdlytrue77@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok in your country), and we do not knowingly process their data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change, and material changes will be noted on this page.
